# TalksNWalks Reference Content Library

This folder is intentionally **not wired into the live posting scripts yet**. The men and children pipelines should be proven first.

## Files

- `books.csv` — 100 original paraphrases inspired by well-known books and leadership/self-development ideas.
- `leaders_classics.csv` — 50 historic/classic items. Short direct quotations are only included when they are verified or come from public-domain/classic sources; the rest are clearly marked `inspired_by`.
- `kids_morals.csv` — 100 original moral/values posts for kids, pre-teens, and teens.

## Content rules

1. Never present an `inspired_by` line as a direct quotation from the author.
2. Direct quotation rows must retain the author/source attribution.
3. Prefer paraphrases for modern copyrighted books.
4. Keep TalksNWalks original content dominant so the page keeps its own voice.
5. Suggested future mix after the audience pipelines are stable:
   - 70% original TalksNWalks content
   - 20% book-inspired / classic-inspired content
   - 10% verified short quotations
6. Kids content should stay age-appropriate, inclusive, non-misogynistic, non-bullying, and respectful of all genders and backgrounds.
7. Before automating any new direct quotation added later, verify the attribution from a reliable primary or institutional source.

## Future integration

When the men/children posting tests are stable, the selector can add `Type`, `Theme`, and `Audience` filters and draw from these files without changing the visual post generator.

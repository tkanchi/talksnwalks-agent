# Talk N Walks — Master Slide Prompts

This document is the source of truth for reusable Instagram slide visual templates.

## Usage rule
Before generating any manual Instagram slide or carousel, present the available styles from this document and ask:

**Which slide style would you like to use for this post?**

Do not generate slides until a style is selected.

## Style 08 — Continuous Janmashtami Scarf Carousel

Use this as the master prompt for generating the 9 individual square slides consistently:

Create a single 1:1 square Instagram carousel slide, 1080×1080, on a pure white background, in a cute polished cartoon/vector style with soft shading, clean outlines, expressive oversized eyes, warm festive colors, and lots of empty space.

This image belongs to a 9-slide continuous Janmashtami carousel. A long golden-yellow dupatta/scarf with tiny subtle dotted details must run horizontally across the slide at the exact same height so that it visually connects seamlessly to the previous and next slides when swiped.

The visual story is: Radha holds one end of the scarf on Slide 1, Krishna holds the other end on Slide 9. Slides 2–8 only show the uninterrupted scarf and occasional small symbolic Krishna elements. No hands, arms, or partial people may appear on Slides 2–8.

Keep the composition extremely minimal and similar to a playful continuous-line cartoon carousel: mostly white background, one central horizontal scarf, one small character/object at most, and tiny floating hearts only where appropriate.

Radha design: very cute youthful cartoon Radha, large expressive brown eyes, sweet innocent face, small red bindi clearly visible on forehead, long dark wavy hair, pink traditional outfit with subtle gold trim, delicate jewelry and flowers in her hair.

Little Krishna design: adorable baby Krishna, blue skin, large expressive brown eyes, curly dark hair, peacock feather, tiny golden crown/jewelry, yellow dhoti, playful innocent expression.

Do not add slide numbers. Do not add borders. Do not add dialogue. Do not add captions except on the final slide. Do not place any watermark or username. Keep the same character design, scarf thickness, scarf color, line height, illustration style and proportions across all 9 slides.

Then add the relevant slide instruction to the end:
- Slide 1: Radha sits at the far left, dreamy and cute, holding the beginning of the golden scarf. Small red bindi clearly visible. A few tiny pink hearts. The scarf exits perfectly through the right edge.
- Slide 2: Only the golden scarf crossing the image continuously from left edge to right edge. Nothing else.
- Slide 3: Only the continuous golden scarf, with a gentle natural wave. Nothing else.
- Slide 4: Only the continuous golden scarf. No hands, no characters, no objects.
- Slide 5: Continuous golden scarf with one small elegant peacock feather resting lightly on or just above the scarf and one tiny heart.
- Slide 6: Only the continuous golden scarf. Keep it minimal.
- Slide 7: Continuous scarf with one cute traditional clay butter pot placed close to the scarf, with a little butter visible. No hands or people.
- Slide 8: Only the continuous golden scarf, creating anticipation before Krishna is revealed. Absolutely no hand visible.
- Slide 9: Adorable little Krishna sitting at the right side holding the end of the same golden scarf. Add a small butter pot, flute, peacock feather and very light festive bunting above. Keep lots of white space. Add only the text “Happy Janmashtami” in a cute warm handwritten-style font near the bottom.

**Critical consistency rule:** the scarf must enter and exit at exactly the same vertical position and thickness on every slide.
